The ScummVM Team is pleased to announce that _Amerzone: The Explorer’s Legacy_, _Loch Ness_, and _The Messenger_ / _Louvre: The Final Curse_ are ready for public testing!

In _Amerzone: The Explorer’s Legacy_, an old explorer’s final wish sends a young journalist to a hidden land with some precious cargo: the stolen egg of the legendary White Birds. Sail, drive, and puzzle your way through Benoît Sokal’s world of strange wildlife, rusting machinery, and forgotten history.

_Loch Ness_ takes private investigator Alan P. Cameron to the Scottish Highlands, where ghostly apparitions trouble Devil’s Ridge Manor—and his client has vanished. Search for clues, uncover a secret laboratory, and keep an eye on the loch. Some mysteries lurk beneath the surface.

In _The Messenger_/ _Louvre: The Final Curse_, agent Morgane Sinclair must recover four dangerous artefacts before the Black Templars can unleash their power. Travel through different eras of the Louvre’s history, mixing modern gadgets with ancient magic. This museum visit comes with considerably more at stake than the admission fee.

All three games run on the _PhoenixVR engine_, also used by _Dracula: Resurrection_ and _Dracula 2: The Last Sanctuary_.

To join the testing, grab a recent [daily development build](https://www.scummvm.org/downloads/#daily) and your original game data files. Report any bugs on our [issue tracker](https://bugs.scummvm.org/), including your ScummVM version, game edition and language, and steps to reproduce the problem.

Mind the egg, watch the water, and try not to end the world. Happy testing!
