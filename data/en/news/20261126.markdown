Having just welcomed _Hollywood Monsters_ by Péndulo Studios, the ScummVM team is pleased to announce that Péndulo’s first adventure, _Igor: Objective Uikokahonia_ (1994), is also ready for public testing!

It all starts when Igor overhears Philip, the college jock, chatting with Laura about the school trip to Uikokahonia. Igor, who is head over heels for Laura, is not about to let that idiot take her on that trip while he stays behind, so he vows to earn his own ticket to Uikokahonia.

All known versions of the game are supported: the Spanish CD-ROM version, which features full voice acting, the Spanish and English floppy versions, and the English demos (versions 1.00s and 1.10s). As usual, you will need the original game data files to play.

To join the test, grab a recent
[daily development build](https://www.scummvm.org/downloads/#daily), add the
game directory to ScummVM, and play. You will need your original game files.
Please report any problems you find on our
[issue tracker](https://bugs.scummvm.org/), following the
[bug reporting guidelines](https://wiki.scummvm.org/index.php?title=Reporting_Bugs).

Will you help igor secure a trip to Uikokahonia?
